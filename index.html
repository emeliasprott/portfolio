About

Where data meets

I'm a computational social scientist trained at UC Berkeley, focused on the intersection of machine learning, causal inference, and political economy.

I like building end-to-end: structuring messy data, modeling the dynamics underneath it, and producing findings that are actually useful to the people who need them. The problems I find most interesting are the ones where incentives and institutional structure matter just as much as the model itself.
