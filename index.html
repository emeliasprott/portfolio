Computational Social Scientist & Data Scientist

About

I'm a computational social scientist trained at UC Berkeley, focused on turning complex, messy data into models and tools that support real-world decision-making. I design data-driven systems that explain how people, incentives, and institutions interact.

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, social science, and human behavior. I'm especially interested in problems where context, incentives, and institutional structure matter just as much as the model itself.

I enjoy building end-to-end systems: from structuring fragmented data, to modeling relationships and dynamics, to producing insights that are interpretable, defensible, and genuinely useful.
